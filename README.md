## 👋 About Me

I'm a **Data and AI Engineer** at **Data n Dashboards Limited**, a New Zealand-based analytics consultancy, working remotely from **Karachi, Pakistan 🇵🇰**. I build production-grade AI agents, retrieval systems and data pipelines, and I like making them useful for real teams, not just impressive in demos.

I graduated **top of my batch** from **NED University of Engineering & Technology** (Computer & Information Systems Engineering) with a **CGPA of 3.967 / 4.00**, a **Silver Medal**, and a full merit scholarship.

- 🤖 **Working on:** LLM agents, RAG pipelines and MCP (Model Context Protocol) servers
- 🔄 **Data engineering:** Apache Airflow and Snowflake ETL, Tableau integration
- 🤝 **Open to:** collaborating on open-source projects in AI and data
- 💬 **Ask me about:** Python, LangGraph, RAG, data engineering, Kaggle
- 🌍 **Languages:** English (IELTS Academic 7.5, C1) · Urdu
